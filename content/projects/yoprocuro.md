## What it was

YoProcuro was a project I started at university.

The idea was to create a platform connecting lawyers with law students so lawyers could access affordable and efficient paralegal services, while students could get practical legal work experience.

## Why it matters

It was an early founder project at the intersection of law, access, operations, and marketplace design.

## What it is

Atlaset is a visual asset-mapping tool for people whose financial life is spread across banks, platforms, entities, trusts, countries, and asset classes.

The goal is not another spreadsheet or generic wealth dashboard. The goal is a clear map of what exists, where it sits, how it connects, and what needs to be protected.

## What it helps with

- Mapping accounts, assets, entities, debts, and structures.
- Seeing relationships across jurisdictions and ownership layers.
- Planning with a fuller picture of the user's financial life.
- Preserving access and continuity for family or trusted contacts.
- Keeping sensitive information private through client-side encryption.

## Positioning

Atlaset is a tool for organization, visibility, and continuity. It does not move money or provide financial, legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice.

## What it was

Settler was a project for mediating workplace problems between employees.

The idea came from seeing firsthand how internal conflicts could escalate when HR was expected to resolve them while also being part of the company structure. Settler was meant to offer a more impartial process for handling those problems before they became worse.

## Why it matters

It was an earlier founder project at the intersection of workplace systems, institutional incentives, conflict resolution, and practical operations.

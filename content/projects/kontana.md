## What it is

Kontana is a free, privacy-first cash tracker built for people who use cash.

It does not connect to bank accounts, aggregate financial accounts, or send spending data to a cloud backend. The product is designed around simple cash logging, on-device storage, offline use, and optional exports.

## What it helps with

- Logging cash spending quickly.
- Reviewing categories and trends.
- Building better spending habits without bank linking.
- Keeping financial data on the user's own device.
- Exporting only when the user chooses.

## Why it matters

Kontana reflects a recurring theme in my work: practical tools that reduce dependency, preserve privacy, and stay simple enough for real use.
